# Credits

A [Minus 80 Studios](../../studio/) game. Built with Phaser 4, TypeScript, and Vite.

## Science

Inspired by Stein, I. R., & Cardon, Z. G. (2026), _New Phytologist_ 252(5): 1832–1842
(doi:10.1111/nph.71593), and the Marine Biological Laboratory news story by Camille Ledoux
(19 Nov 2026). The optodes used in the original study were made by hand by Suzanne Thomas (MBL), per the
paper's acknowledgements. See [`REFERENCES.md`](REFERENCES.md) and
[`SCIENTIST_PROFILES.md`](SCIENTIST_PROFILES.md).

MUCK LIFE is inspired by this research. It was not developed with, or endorsed by, the researchers or
the Marine Biological Laboratory. Biographies, photos, and personal statements are pending written
approval, per studio policy.

## Art

All visuals are original placeholders drawn procedurally at runtime (Phaser Graphics and Text). No
external image or font files are bundled. See [`ARTIST_BRIEF.md`](ARTIST_BRIEF.md) for the planned
illustration pass.

## Audio

All music, effects, and ambience are synthesized live with the Web Audio API (`src/audio/Sound.ts`). No
sampled or licensed audio.
