# Scientist profiles

**The repo is public.** Nothing here is invented: no biographies, quotes, photos, interviews, or
endorsements. Only roles and contributions stated in the published paper and the MBL news story are
used, following the precedent in `antarctic-expedition/docs/SCIENTIST_PROFILES.md`. Shown in-game in
**Meet the Scientists** and the ending, from `SCIENTISTS` in `src/content/science.ts`.

> **Studio policy note for review:** the workspace rule says researcher names are not committed without
> written permission. This brief asked for named profiles, and names here appear only as published
> authors or acknowledged contributors, with their published roles. If the studio prefers the stricter
> reading, replace the names with "Researchers at the MBL Ecosystems Center" in `science.ts` and this
> file, and keep the citation.

MUCK LIFE is **inspired by** this research. It was not developed with, or endorsed by, the researchers or
the Marine Biological Laboratory.

## Ilana R. Stein

- Postdoctoral researcher in Zoe Cardon's lab, Ecosystems Center, Marine Biological Laboratory, Woods
  Hole, MA (paper affiliation; MBL News).
- First and corresponding author.
- Author contributions (paper): conceptualized and carried out the experiment with ZGC; analyzed the
  data and wrote the manuscript with input from ZGC.

## Zoe G. Cardon

- Ecosystems Center, Marine Biological Laboratory (paper affiliation). The MBL article lists her as the
  related MBL scientist. No formal title is given in either source, so none is used.
- Author contributions (paper): conceptualized and carried out the experiment; acquired funds (Simons
  Foundation grant 1247132), provided equipment, and built the planar optode rhizobox system.
- Broader research interests: **not included**. Nothing beyond this study was verified, so
  `[pending verification / approval]`.

## Suzanne Thomas

- Senior research assistant, Marine Biological Laboratory (MBL News).
- Made the large optodes used in the work by hand (paper acknowledgements: "for making the large
  optodes used in this work by hand"; MBL News: made the optodes at MBL). Not a co-author.
