# Playtesting — Queenless

## How to run a session

1. Build or run the current version (`npm run dev`, or share a `dist/` build).
2. Give testers **no instructions** beyond "play however you like." Watch silently.
3. Note where they hesitate, misunderstand Buzz Pulse's effect, or stop watching their nestmates entirely.
4. Ask the debrief questions, then log results below.

## Debrief questions

- What do you think Buzz Pulse actually does? (The design only succeeds if players describe something close to
  "makes nearby bees pay attention," not "makes bees do what I want.")
- Did you notice your nestmates had their own personalities / tendencies, or did they feel random?
- Did you notice anything happen to Queenie specifically? When?
- (After the ending) What did you learn about what happens to a bee colony when the queen is gone? Did that feel
  like a lesson, or part of the game?

## Accessibility checks per session

Keyboard-only · gamepad-only · touch-only · sound off · colour-blind simulation · reduced motion · small screen
