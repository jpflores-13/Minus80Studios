# Ending Sequence: "Did You Know?" (Minus 80 Studios format)

Every -80 game ends with a short, skippable discovery sequence (target: 20–30s of reading, longer if the player
lingers). Reached from a cleared level's or Queenless Mode's results screen ("EXPLORE THE SCIENCE"), or directly
from the title screen's "DID YOU KNOW?" button.

Rules: all text is drawn from `docs/SCIENCE.md`'s Verified statements; any player-facing claim not traceable to a
row in that table needs a citation added first, not just prose. Repeat players can skip ahead with any key or a
tap; Esc returns to the title at any point. The sequence is reachable from both the title screen and every
results screen, win or lose.
