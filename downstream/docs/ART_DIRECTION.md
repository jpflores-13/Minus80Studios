# Art Direction — Downstream!

## Mood

Bright, readable, and hopeful but honest. A living river worth protecting, with plastic that stands out clearly against nature.

## Current placeholder palette (`src/config/gameConfig.ts`)

| Name       | Hex       | Use             |
| ---------- | --------- | --------------- |
| land       | `#3d5a3a` | Riverbanks      |
| water      | `#2f6f8f` | River           |
| waterLight | `#5fa3bf` | Ripples         |
| bottle     | `#f2f2f2` | Bottle debris   |
| bag        | `#f0c94a` | Bag debris      |
| fragment   | `#e0715a` | Fragment debris |

## Principles

- **Debris is distinguished by shape, not just colour** (bottle, bag, fragment), for colour-blind players.
- **Top-down, map-like clarity.** Players must read flow direction and speed at a glance.
- **Respect reduced motion.** Ripples stop moving when the OS asks.
- **Original or licensed only.** Log every external asset in `ASSET_ATTRIBUTION.md`.
