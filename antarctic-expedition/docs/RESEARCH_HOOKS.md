# Research hooks

Ways a real polar/marine researcher (starting with, but not limited to, the scientists already cited)
could extend this game, if they chose to get involved.

- **Approve a real profile.** If Dr. Kienle or any co-author on the cited papers wants to contribute a
  real biography, photo, or quote, that replaces the bracketed placeholder in `src/content/science.ts`
  and `docs/SCIENTIST_PROFILES.md` — see that doc's "Adding a real profile later" section.
- **Add a seventh mystery.** `src/content/mysteries.ts` is a flat array; a new finding with a real DOI
  slots in the same way as the existing six, with its own required Journal evidence and calibrated
  interpretation.
- **Expand the Discovery Journal.** `src/content/journal.ts` currently covers nine species/specimens.
  Any additional DIVE wildlife or ONE DROP specimen (`content/wildlife.ts`, `content/organisms.ts`) can
  get a full Journal entry once a researcher supplies or confirms its facts and sources.
- **Correct or deepen an existing entry.** Every fact in the Journal and in `docs/SCIENCE.md` is meant to
  be checked against the cited source; a researcher spotting an oversimplification should flag it against
  `docs/SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md`'s claim table.
- **Suggest a seventh biological scale or new instrument**, if there's a real one between viral and
  molecular, or beyond molecular, worth representing — see `docs/INSTRUMENT_DATABASE.md` for the current
  six.
