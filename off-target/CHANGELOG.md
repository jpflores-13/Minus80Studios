# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First playable: six stages, heroes ACE, BLOCK, LOCK and FREEZE, bosses CHOP and SNIP, GUIDE and GENE.
- Platforming with ladders, ledges, moving platforms, gates, goo, bouncers, rollers and boss shockwaves.
- Power-ups: sprint, shield, double jump, slow motion, passage key. Checkpoints and quick restarts.
- Casual, Arcade and Precision difficulty; local high scores, stars and best times.
- Opening scene, results, ending (science text pending), settings, keyboard, gamepad and touch.
