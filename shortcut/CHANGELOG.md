# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Levels 4–8: Power Play (wrong connections), Rush Hour (trucks that shove machine ends), Nothing Lasts Forever
  (maintenance, depot speed and fleet from a budget), Heat Advisory (a mid-level weather event and a building that
  opens late), A New Identity (same road, new town).
- The ending reveal: the city crossfades into a science view of the same running simulation, then short pages
  on the science and where the analogy breaks down. Reference placeholders only.
- Science Mode, City Planner (sandbox with map comparison and data export) and Blueprint (match a target map).
- City Connectivity Map now uses an effective-distance contact model, so it shows a diagonal, dots, stripes and
  domains.
- Undo and Reset while planning; larger touch targets with a nudge-and-confirm picker; new building art.

## 0.1.0

- Stochastic shortcut engine: depots, machines with independent ends, random pace and lifetimes, directional
  checkpoints that can leak, collisions between machines, edges of the map, and a time-averaged connectivity map.
- Probabilistic deliveries, consumer uptime, and district mix-ups, all scored over the whole minute.
- Levels 1–3: The Long Way Home, One Way Street, Good Neighbors.
- City Connectivity Map on the results screen.
- Keyboard play, screen-reader announcements, large text, reduced effects, touch, and phone layouts.
