# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Stochastic shortcut engine: depots, machines with independent ends, random pace and lifetimes, directional
  checkpoints that can leak, collisions between machines, edges of the map, and a time-averaged connectivity map.
- Probabilistic deliveries, consumer uptime, and district mix-ups, all scored over the whole minute.
- Levels 1–3: The Long Way Home, One Way Street, Good Neighbors.
- City Connectivity Map on the results screen.
- Keyboard play, screen-reader announcements, large text, reduced effects, touch, and phone layouts.
