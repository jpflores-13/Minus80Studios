# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- The hero now wraps around the arena edges instead of getting cornered against the membrane wall, matching
  Snail Assassin's reef.
- The opening now names and describes the Wave 1 enemies (Cutter, Stalker, Swarm) instead of showing
  unlabeled silhouettes.
- A real "DID YOU KNOW?" science reveal with checked citations (Jinek 2012, Bondy-Denomy 2013, Dong 2017),
  replacing the placeholder text; reachable from the title screen at any time, not just after winning.

## 0.1.0

- First playable: eight campaign waves (Infiltration to Host Takeover) with MEGA CUTTER and HOST DEFENCE PRIME.
- Six enemy types, four weapons with a configurable compatibility table, inhibited states shown by shape.
- ACR Cloud and Phage Reinforcements; nine stacking upgrades; resources; obstacles and crowded zones.
- Endless Survival, a local Daily Challenge (same seed for everyone each day), high scores.
- Opening scene, results summary, settings, touch, keyboard and gamepad.
- Object pooling for enemies, shots, pickups and obstacles.
