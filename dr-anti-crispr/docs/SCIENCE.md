# DR. ANTI-CRISPR — Science notes

The game is fiction inspired by the arms race between phages and bacterial CRISPR systems. Science words are kept
out of play; the ending (`src/content/science.ts`) holds the explanatory pages.

## Status

- The ending's explanatory text is a **placeholder**, to be written with the contributing scientist and checked
  by a reviewer. The game shows whatever is in `src/content/science.ts`.
- Every `inspiredBy` label in `src/content/arsenal.ts` is marked **REVIEW**. PULSE, ACR CLOUD, Phage
  Reinforcements, the bosses and all upgrades are fictional.
- Damage, timers, odds, durations and compatibility are gameplay values, not measurements.
