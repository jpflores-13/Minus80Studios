# DR. ANTI-CRISPR — Science notes

The game is fiction inspired by the arms race between phages and bacterial CRISPR systems. Science words are kept
out of play during the run; the ending (`src/content/science.ts`, the "DID YOU KNOW?" reveal in
`EndingScene.ts`) holds the explanatory pages, with real, checked citations.

## Status

- The ending now carries real scientific content (not placeholders) for "The Science Behind the Game" and "An
  Arms Race, Not a Single Trick," each with checked references. "Meet the Scientist," "Credits," and the
  name/biography lines remain **placeholders** pending the contributing scientist's written approval, per this
  repo's public-credit rule.
- Every `inspiredBy` label in `src/content/arsenal.ts` is still marked **REVIEW**. PULSE, ACR CLOUD, Phage
  Reinforcements, the bosses and all upgrades are fictional.
- Damage, timers, odds, durations and compatibility remain gameplay values, not measurements.

## What the reveal does not claim

- That an intact phage walks, fires projectiles, or makes decisions inside a bacterium — it's a molecular
  process, not a tiny soldier.
- That any one anti-CRISPR mechanism (e.g., DNA mimicry by AcrIIA4) applies to every weapon in the game; each
  weapon's specific real-world mechanism is marked REVIEW until checked.
- That cooperative phage effects (Phage Reinforcements) are universal or work identically across every
  CRISPR system — this ability is an abstract, fictional gameplay adaptation.
